# Vaultlight: Campaign Trajectory (Distilled)

## 1) Macro Arcs

### Arc A — Pre‑Discovery Heist (Act 1: “The Penumbra Job”)
**Core shape:** desperate specialists → tempted by access + money → penetrate the Penumbra’s ecosystem → learn the building is deeper than anyone admits → assemble the heist plan → breach Vault 9.

**Primary beats (high level):**
- **Montrose signals:** each PC receives a tailored “1108” breadcrumb.
- **Suite 1108:** velvet boxes (cash + Penumbra cards) + Mr. Nobody broadcast; crew accepts the job.
- **Penumbra casing:** first taste of private floors and backrooms; build contacts/heat; identify security layers.
- **Descent logic:** discover the sub‑basement structure (B3/B4/B5) and that **Vault 9 is on B5**.
- **Heist plan locked:** gather the missing blueprint segments / access procedures; choose approach vectors; set contingencies.
- **Breach:** the vault opens; **Discovery** is released/encountered (the embodiment, not an artifact).

**End-state of Arc A:** the crew gets what they came for (opening Vault 9), but the act of opening it changes the campaign’s reality.

---

### Arc B — Post‑Discovery Panic (Act 2: “After the Door Opens”)
**Core shape:** the crew survives the breach → the world reacts to Discovery being loose → the Penumbra turns from setting to predator → choices become damage-control.

**Primary beats (high level):**
- **Immediate fallout:** alarms (literal and social), missing time, strange attention, contradictory evidence, “impossible coincidences.”
- **Heat escalation:** security, rivals, and unseen forces converge; neutral ground becomes contested ground.
- **Wonder vs. Consequence:** Mr. Nobody’s delight collides with the cost of what’s been unleashed.
- **Containment / bargaining:** the crew must decide whether to return/contain Discovery, use it, hide it, sell it, or ally with it.
- **Endgame:** consequences cascade—relationships fracture, the city’s power map redraws, and the crew either reshapes the new normal or is crushed by it.

**End-state of Arc B:** the crew either (a) stabilizes the situation at great cost, (b) rides the chaos to reshape Lake City, or (c) becomes the cautionary tale the city tells in whispers.

## 2) Primary Characters (Principals / Embodiments)

### **Discovery (Embodiment; contained in Vault 9)**
- **Role:** the campaign’s turning point; the thing that makes “the heist” stop being about money and start being about reality.
- **Vibe:** curiosity made dangerous; knowledge that spreads; doors that won’t stay closed once opened.
- **Function in play:** creates new leads faster than the crew can control; destabilizes routines and “known” maps.

### **Mr. Nobody (Embodiment of Wonder; the benefactor)**
- **Role:** tempter, patron, and catalyst; pushes PCs toward the unprecedented.
- **Operating style:** intimate, delighted, invitational pressure rather than threats; communicates via broadcasts/signals.
- **Agenda:** open the door; get the crew to *choose* the impossible; keep the city from dying of predictability.
- **Constraint:** can smooth their path up to the threshold, but (per Session 2 recap) cannot go inside the Penumbra.

### **Consequence (Embodiment; anticipated antagonist / counterweight)**
- **Role:** the bill coming due; the force that insists actions matter, costs accumulate, and escapes close.
- **Function in play:** turns “cool successes” into ongoing problems; makes the city remember what the crew did.

### **(Optional / implied) Other forces**
- **Security & management hierarchy** within the Penumbra (human systems that behave like a machine).
- **Rivals, high-rollers, fixers** who see the crew as assets or threats once they gain access.
- **The city itself** as a pressure system: rainy-season noir, soft surveillance, rumor economies.

## 3) Primary Locations

### **The Montrose Hotel (Suite 1108)**
- **Role:** recruitment and threshold; the first “chosen room.”
- **Function:** brings the crew together; delivers cash + Penumbra access; stage for Mr. Nobody’s first contact.

### **The Penumbra (Underground casino / neutral ground)**
- **Role:** main setpiece location for Arc A; becomes hostile territory in Arc B.
- **Texture:** velvet darkness, neon spill, smoky haze; jazz + synth; mirrored walls; hidden doors; back corridors that “change.”
- **Zones:** public casino/bar; private rooms; high-roller floors; back corridors.

### **The Vault Basements (structural secret of the Penumbra)**
- **Known behind the screen:**  
  - **B2:** Vaults 1–3, containing more mundane valuables, records, and dangerous knowledge  
  - **B3:** Vaults 4–6  
  - **B4/B5:** the deeper restricted vault architecture, culminating in **Vault 9** on **B5**
- **Role:** the descent; each deeper level is a tighter reality with stricter access, more watchers, and weirder infrastructure.

### **Iron District**
- **Role:** noir pressure cooker; labor, warehouses, diner static, cheap jobs, and routes to back-of-house access.

### **Lake City (setting-as-machine)**
- **Role:** omnipresent mood and constraint; “The Rainy Season” as emotional physics.

## 4) Theme / Setting

### Setting pillars
- **Noir heist in a rain-soaked city**: desperation, debt, leverage, secrets traded like currency.
- **Supernatural-adjacent, not overt fantasy**: the uncanny emerges as patterns, signals, impossible coincidences, and entities-as-ideas.
- **Neutral ground is never neutral**: the Penumbra is a truce line with teeth.

### Thematic pillars
- **Wonder vs. Consequence**: the desire to open doors vs. the cost of opening them.
- **Discovery as contagion**: knowledge/novelty spreads; once seen, can’t be unseen.
- **Choice under pressure**: Fate-style compels—people don’t just suffer consequences, they *take them on*.

### FATE tone fit (table-facing)
- **Aspects** should read like hooks: “Neutral Ground with Hidden Doors,” “The Rainy Season,” “Access Bought in Velvet and Brass.”
- **Compels** should feel like noir gravity: the city pushes back, security notices, favors come due.
- **Success with cost** should be common: every win creates a new vulnerability.
