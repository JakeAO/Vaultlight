**Role:** Senior Custodian / Crew Manager

## Description
Etta Lorne has worked at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}) for decades, building a custodial operation based on repetition, standards, and close management rather than heroics. Aged but still in excellent shape, she runs refresh schedules, staff expectations, and surface maintenance with the kind of practical authority that makes everyone around her sharper.

Etta is entirely non-supernatural and has no awareness of the paranormal forces moving around the casino. Her competence is human, learned, and earned the slow way.

## Appearance
- Gray hair pulled into a tight bun
- An extremely clean work uniform with not a wrinkle or stain out of place
- A steady, practical expression and posture that signal total control of her domain

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), public restrooms, service corridors, custodial stations, and supply rooms
- **Connections:** Custodial crews, maintenance workers, floor staff, long-serving Penumbra employees
- **Goals:** Keep the casino visibly spotless, keep her staff disciplined, and prevent small maintenance problems from becoming operational issues
- **Threat Level:** Low direct threat, moderate observational risk in any infiltration involving public facilities or service corridors

## Relationships
Caleb's cover story during Session 3 amused Etta more than it convinced her. She accepted his explanation because it was plausible enough and because she preferred to keep an eye on him rather than escalate immediately.

## Aspects
- ***"Cleanliness Is a System"***
- ***"People Are Easier to Manage Than Messes"***
- ***"Nothing Supernatural About Excellence"***
