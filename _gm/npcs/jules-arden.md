**Role:** Cocktail Server / Accidental Informant

## Description
Jules Arden is a young cocktail server at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), normally deployed to private-floor service but reassigned to the public lounge whenever staffing conflicts hit. He is personable, observant, and far too responsive to confident authority figures who imply he could profit from what he knows.

Jules is not malicious and does not think of himself as disloyal. He simply has weak filters under pressure and a habit of mistaking attention for trust.

## Appearance
- Crisp service uniform and polished shoes, meticulously maintained
- Alert posture that shifts between practiced hospitality and visible nerves
- A tray hand that stays steady even when his voice does not

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), public lounge, private-floor service lanes, and staff corridors
- **Connections:** Bartenders, servers, shift supervisors, regular lounge patrons
- **Goals:** Keep his shifts, get promoted back to preferred assignments, and find ways to turn inside knowledge into personal advantage
- **Threat Level:** Low direct threat, moderate leak risk for operational chatter and staffing movements

## Relationships
Robert hooked Jules quickly by posing as a private investigator tied to a law firm, implying there might be money in insider information. Jules became a useful source on daily labor movement to the Penumbra basement.
