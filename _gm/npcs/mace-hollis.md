**Role:** Loading Dock Manager / Logistics Enforcer

## Description
Mace Hollis manages [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}) loading docks like a military timetable, with clipboards, spreadsheets, and zero tolerance for improvisation. He is blunt, difficult, and often rude under pressure, but extremely competent at keeping goods, labor, and schedules aligned in a chaotic environment.

Mace is not paranormal and has no awareness of the supernatural undercurrents around the casino. He reads every disruption through a purely operational lens: staffing problems, bad manifests, late trucks, or sloppy supervisors.

## Appearance
- Wrinkled button-down shirt with rolled sleeves and a loosened tie
- Ink-stained fingers and coffee rings on his paperwork
- A constant stack of clipboards tracking shifts, manifests, route timing, and dock utilization by the hour

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), loading docks, receiving corridors, and inventory cage
- **Connections:** Dock crews, temp labor agencies, delivery drivers, warehouse clerks, shift security at service entrances
- **Goals:** Keep cargo flow predictable, eliminate delays, and avoid any disruption that draws management scrutiny
- **Threat Level:** Low physical threat, moderate logistical leverage over access and movement in back-of-house areas

## Relationships
Mace previously fired Evan from the loading docks due to reliability concerns, not personal hostility. He considers Evan capable in short bursts but too inconsistent for critical schedule-bound work.
