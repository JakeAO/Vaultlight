**Role:** First Aide Officer / Quiet Stabilizer

## Description
Tamsin Ward serves as the daytime first aide officer at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), treating injured patrons and staff before incidents can spill into public view. A former Army Ranger and field medic with several combat tours behind them, they are efficient, unsentimental, and difficult to rattle. Tamsin does not romanticize violence, but they understand it intimately and know how fast a manageable wound can become a dead body, a police report, or both.

## Appearance
- Neatly rolled sleeves over forearms marked by old service scars
- A compact medical bag stocked with field dressings, painkillers, and trauma supplies
- Steady gray eyes and a voice that stays level even when everyone else is panicking

## Known Information
- **Pronouns:** They/Them
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), staff clinic, gaming floors, and back-of-house corridors
- **Connections:** Penumbra security, floor staff, custodial crews, loading dock workers, discreet private physicians
- **Goals:** Keep people alive, keep incidents contained, and keep the authorities out of Penumbra business
- **Threat Level:** Low offensive danger, high stabilizing value in any crisis inside the casino

## Relationships
Tamsin previously worked alongside Evan during one of his earlier temporary jobs at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), when he was assigned to the loading docks. They remember him as anxious but reliable when given a clear task and enough room to work.

- ***"Patch It Up, Keep It Moving"***
