**Role:** Head of Cashiering / Transfer-Cage Commander

## Description
Rina Volkov controls [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }})'s cash handling ecosystem: staffing transfer cages, supervising count integrity, and locking down movement between counting rooms and vault access points. Her management style is punitive, exacting, and openly violent when she believes workers are threatening operational reliability.

Rina is human, but unlike most people in Lake City, she retained awareness of the paranormal world despite the imprisonment of Discovery. She does not advertise that fact, and she does not discuss it unless strategically useful.

## Appearance
- Severe posture and an expression that reads like a warning
- Crisp dark workwear with sleeves rolled for long shifts in the money room
- Ledger pencil, key ring, and transfer manifests always within reach

## Known Information
- **Background:** Orphan of the Soviet States
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), transfer cages, counting rooms, and vault access corridors
- **Connections:** Cashiers, armored-transfer handlers, shift security, vault clerks, select Penumbra executives
- **Goals:** Eliminate variance in money movement, keep vault flows predictable, and maintain authority through fear and competence
- **Threat Level:** Moderate direct danger, high procedural control over anyone trying to move near cash pathways

## Relationships
Rina has little patience for personalities and evaluates people almost entirely by reliability under pressure. She can become a major obstacle to any heist plan that depends on timing, because she is the one person most likely to notice when a sequence is off by even a minute.
