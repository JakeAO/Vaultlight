**Role:** Penumbra First Aide Officer

## Description
Tamsin Ward serves as the daytime first aide officer at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), treating injured patrons and staff before trouble becomes official. A former Army Ranger and combat medic with several tours behind them, they carry themselves with calm efficiency and the hard-earned patience of someone who has seen much worse than a casino panic.

## Appearance
- Neatly rolled sleeves over a forearm marked by old service scars
- A compact medical bag that never seems far from reach
- Steady gray eyes and a voice that stays level under pressure

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), staff clinic, gaming floors, and back-of-house corridors
- **Connections:** Penumbra security, floor staff, custodial crews, loading dock workers

## Relationships
Tamsin previously worked alongside Evan during one of his earlier temporary jobs at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), when he was assigned to the loading docks.
