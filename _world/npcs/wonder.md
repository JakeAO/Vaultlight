**Alias:** Mr Nobody
**Role:** Benefactor / Tempter / Embodiment of Wonder

## Description
Mr. Nobody is Wonder in a tailored, conversational shape: the appetite for the unprecedented dressed up as a patient patron with excellent timing. He does not rule by force when he can instead make the impossible feel like the most irresistible option in the room.

He communicates through broadcasts, phones, speakers, screens, and other mediated thresholds because Wonder works best when it arrives as invitation rather than intrusion. He presses with warmth, intimacy, and selective truth, rewarding momentum toward the unknown while withholding anything that would make the choice feel safe or ordinary. He wants Discovery unleashed because a city starved of surprise is already half-dead, but that desire does not make him trustworthy.
