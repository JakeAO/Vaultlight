**Role:** Head of Cashiering

## Description
Rina Volkov runs money operations at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}) with cold precision and a temper like broken glass. She does not care about apologies, excuses, or formalities; she expects workers to execute correctly the first time, every time. Those who fail her standards learn quickly that Rina's discipline is personal, immediate, and often physical.

## Appearance
- Severe posture and an expression that reads like a warning
- Crisp dark workwear with sleeves rolled for long shifts in the money room
- Ledger pencil, key ring, and transfer manifests always within reach

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), transfer cages, counting rooms, and vault access corridors
- **Connections:** Cashiers, armored-transfer handlers, shift security, vault clerks

## Relationships
Rina oversees the workers who move money between transfer cages and the vault. Staff in her orbit either become disciplined fast or disappear from the roster.

## Aspects
- ***"Do It Right the First Time"***
- ***"Every Bill Accounted For"***
