**Role:** Penumbra Loading Dock Manager

## Description
Mace Hollis runs the loading docks at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}) with relentless attention to schedule, inventory, and throughput. Abrasive, demanding, and perpetually stressed about timelines, Mace treats every delayed pallet like a personal insult and every unlogged crate like a crisis.

## Appearance
- Wrinkled button-down shirt with rolled sleeves and a loosened tie
- Ink-stained fingers from constant note-taking and revisions
- Always carrying a stack of clipboards packed with shift plans, manifests, and minute operational details

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), loading docks, receiving corridors, and inventory cage
- **Connections:** Dock crews, temp labor agencies, delivery drivers, warehouse clerks

## Relationships
Mace fired Evan the last time Evan worked the loading docks. There is no personal vendetta behind it: Mace simply believes Evan is unreliable in a role that depends on strict timing and consistency.

## Aspects
- ***"Every Delay Costs Someone"***
- ***"Clipboards Over Gut Instinct"***
