**Role:** High-Stakes Gambler

## Description
"Silk" Rainer is a familiar sight in the second-floor private gaming room at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), where he plays for sums that make other wealthy patrons visibly nervous. His background is unknown, his voice rarely rises, and no matter how high the stakes climb, he remains unnervingly calm.

## Appearance
- An impossibly fine gray suit, matched with a gray shirt and tie
- A composed expression that never seems to crack, even mid-hand
- Two massive bodyguards at his shoulders, moving wordlessly like extensions of his will

## Known Information
- **Background:** Unknown
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), private second-floor gaming room
- **Connections:** Penumbra floor staff, private-room dealers, two nameless bodyguards who never leave his side

## Relationships
During Heylorn's reconnaissance of the private gaming floor, Silk sat down to gamble with him despite the absurd gap between Heylorn's modest bets and Silk's usual stakes. The bodyguards stayed close throughout, wordlessly managing chips, drinks, and anything else Silk required before he ever had to ask.

## Aspects
- ***"Impossibly Calm at Any Stakes"***
- ***"Always the Biggest Bet at the Table"***
