**Role:** Cocktail Server

## Description
Jules Arden is a young cocktail server at [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), usually assigned to the private floor but occasionally bumped to the public lounge when scheduling gets messy. Friendly, eager, and not nearly as cautious as his elders, Jules is the sort of employee who can be talked into sharing more than he should with the right story and the right promised payoff.

## Appearance
- Crisp service uniform worn with slightly nervous pride
- Quick smile and quicker hands balancing trays through crowded rooms
- Restless eyes that light up whenever gossip sounds profitable

## Known Information
- **Location:** [The Penumbra]({{ '/world/locations/the-penumbra/' | relative_url }}), public lounge and private-floor service rotation
- **Connections:** Bartenders, cocktail servers, lounge regulars, private-floor patrons

## Relationships
During Robert's Session 3 investigation, Jules became his easiest in-house source after Robert fed him a believable lie about legal investigators and payout opportunities for useful insider details.

- ***"Young, Eager, and Underpaid"***
- ***"Knows Just Enough to Be Dangerous"***
