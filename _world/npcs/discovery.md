**Alias:** D
**Role:** Embodiment / Turning Point / Living Threshold

## Description
Discovery is not best understood as a person, artifact, or captive. It is the personification of the act of discovering something real and destabilizing: the instant a sealed pattern breaks, a hidden truth becomes visible, and the observer is changed by knowing it.

Its presence can pull up memories, longings, fears, or sudden flashes of impossible clarity simply by being seen. Discovery is curious before it is hostile, intimate before it is grand, and dangerous because contact with it makes people notice more than they were built to bear.

## Appearance
- A figure whose age, gender, and exact features shift with angle, distance, and light
- Eyes, hands, posture, or voice that seem to belong to different people from one glance to the next
- A soft domestic stillness around it that can abruptly become unbearable, as if the room has remembered something first
